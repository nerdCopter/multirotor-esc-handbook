# Takedown Policy

`research/transcripts/` contains auto-generated YouTube caption transcripts for videos cited as sources elsewhere in this repository. They are included for citation, verification, and research purposes — so that a specific technical claim in this knowledge base can be traced back to the exact words a source video actually said, rather than to a paraphrase that could drift from the original over time.

If you are the creator of a cited video, a rights holder, or YouTube itself, and you would like a transcript removed from this repository:

* Open an issue on this repository requesting removal, identifying the specific video(s) by URL or filename, or
* Contact the repository owner directly through GitHub.

Any such request will be honored promptly — the transcript file(s) will be removed. The corresponding citation elsewhere in this repository will still note that the video exists and was used as a source, but will no longer include the verbatim transcript text.

This policy applies specifically to `research/transcripts/`. Other material under `research/` (extracted analysis notes, copies of publicly-available open-source project wiki pages and READMEs) is not verbatim third-party video content and is not covered by this notice, though the same principle applies: raise an issue if you believe something here shouldn't be here, and it will be addressed.
